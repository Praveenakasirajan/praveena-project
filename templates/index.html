💡 Best Practice Answering Tips

1
Clear Structure: State your key technical concept first, then explain the implementation details and tradeoffs.
2
Maintain Eye Contact: Look toward your camera lens to simulate looking directly at your interviewer.
3
Pacing & Clarity: Aim for a steady 120–160 WPM pace. Avoid rushed answers or frequent filler pauses.
4
Specific Depth: Address exact methods, data structures, or engineering choices rather than high-level generalizations.
